# Logo and Favicon Setup Instructions

## Required Image Files

To complete the logo setup, you need to add the following image files to the `/public` folder:

### 1. WHO Somalia Logo (Main Logo)
- **File**: `/public/who-somalia-logo.png`
- **Description**: The full WHO Somalia logo with the UN emblem (globe with Rod of Asclepius) and "World Health Organization Somalia" text
- **Recommended Size**: 200x60px or higher (maintain aspect ratio)
- **Format**: PNG with transparent background preferred
- **Usage**: Displayed in the header/navigation bar

### 2. Favicon Files
Based on the Federal Government of Somalia coat of arms emblem:

- **File**: `/public/favicon.ico`
  - Traditional favicon format
  - Size: 16x16, 32x32, 48x48 (multi-size ICO file)
  - Should use the emblem portion (crown, shield with star, leopards)

- **File**: `/public/favicon-16x16.png`
  - Size: 16x16px
  - PNG format

- **File**: `/public/favicon-32x32.png`
  - Size: 32x32px
  - PNG format

- **File**: `/public/apple-touch-icon.png`
  - Size: 180x180px
  - PNG format
  - For iOS devices

## Image Sources

1. **WHO Somalia Logo**: Use the official WHO Somalia logo image provided
2. **Favicon**: Extract the emblem portion (crown, blue shield with white star, leopards) from the Federal Government of Somalia coat of arms

## Notes

- The favicon should focus on the emblem portion (crown, shield, star) as the text would be illegible at small sizes
- Maintain the blue, white, and gold color scheme from the original emblems
- Ensure images are optimized for web use (compressed but high quality)
